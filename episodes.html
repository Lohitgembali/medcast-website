Dr. Argel Aguilar-Valles

Associate Professor of Neuroscience, Carleton University

How does ketamine actually work in the brain, and can psychedelics change brain structure? Lohit and Jarissan talk with a neuroscientist who studies exactly that: his path from a molecular biology class in Mexico to his own lab, why psychiatric disorders are so hard to study, a surprising discovery about psychedelics and brain myelination, and what he’d tell his undergrad self.
