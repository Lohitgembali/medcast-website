Questions, answered.

How do I join MedCast?

Email medcast.mcmaster@gmail.com with the subject line “MedCast student interest”. Tell us what interests you and how you’d like to contribute.

Is there an application form?

An online interest form is being prepared. Until it’s ready, email is the way to reach us.

What can I help with?

Podcasting, interviewing, research, editing, design, social media, events and outreach.

I’m a researcher. Can I be a guest?

Yes, we’d love to hear from you. Email us with the subject line “MedCast guest inquiry” and include a short description of your work or the conversation you have in mind.

When does recording start?

Our first recordings are planned for November 2026.
